Two views, and the difference between them is deliberate. E-03 ConferenceGrid
One card per conference, grouped under a region caption. It was one card per region with the conferences listed inside and a View region link that went nowhere the card did not already go; a conference is the thing people look for, so it gets the card, and the federations in it are named with el-13 FederationTag at size S. The U21 conferences sit inside their region with the rest — the feed files them all under a "U21" region, which would otherwise collect them into a group of their own. Review was sceptical that a map of 100+ stops helps anyone, so the geography is reduced to five groups with a progress track per conference.

Note
Stats are GP / PPG / APG / RPG, the configuration already in the brand template. The background is a flat grey placeholder — the key visual, the gradient or a photograph all drop into that layer. The card is locked to 1 : 1.4 and everything inside it is positioned in percentages, so it can be dropped into a four-up roster, a three-up rail or a compact list at any width and hold its proportions — type does not scale with the frame, so a density property steps it down instead: default above 300, compact around 240, dense at 180. The name plate is a solid layer carrying the corner cut, not a gradient fade, so it holds up over any photograph, and it is inset equally on the left, right and bottom. The squad number has been removed. On the Efficiency figure used earlier — that came from the written player-page specification rather than from a published feed and could not be verified as an existing field, so it is out until the data source is confirmed.
E-09 FederationDirectory
The Teams index: every federation in the competition, filtered by region. The A–Z jump bar came off — with 67 federations the letter is rarely what anyone knows and the region is — so el-25 AlphaIndex gave way to a row of el-14 Chip at size S under the search field, and the count moved under the chips because it describes what the filter left. The single most common task on the site is finding one federation, and this is the page that has to answer it without a search.
