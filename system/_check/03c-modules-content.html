C-01 AboutBlock
What replaces the hero. The banner copy was to be removed and the explanation moved into a real About section (LP-02, LP-03), so that a visitor can understand how a team reaches the U23 World Cup without asking anyone.
default
What the Nations League is
Young national teams compete in regional conferences around the world. Twenty of them reach the FIBA 3x3 U23 World Cup — the host federation, plus nineteen decided across the season.
How it works
1
Conferences run on their own calendar
Each conference plays six stops. They do not meet each other, and they start and finish weeks apart.
2
Win your conference and you are in
A conference winner qualifies the moment its conference ends.
3
Everyone else races on tour points
The remaining places go to the highest-ranked federations across all conferences.

C-03 PhotoGallery
Two carousel types plus the viewer. Auto advances on its own with a progress indicator and a pause control, and belongs on the landing page. Manual has previous and next plus a position counter, and belongs on a stop or conference page where the fan is actively browsing. Tiles are a fixed 3:2 — the ratio the photography is actually delivered in.

The viewer opens on the whole set and drills into a single photo on tap, which is how Google Photos and Apple Photos behave and is the right order here: a stop produces a few hundred frames and a fan wants to scan before committing to one. How qualification works
Twenty federations reach the FIBA 3x3 U23 World Cup: the host, plus nineteen decided across the season. A conference winner qualifies the moment its conference ends. The remaining places go to the highest-ranked federations on tour points.
